Connect your TikTok account

Clip Studio edits gameplay highlights into short vertical videos. After you connect TikTok, each finished clip is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You get a notification in the TikTok app, review the draft, write or paste the caption and post it yourself. Nothing is published without you.

Permissions requested
  • user.info.basic — to show which TikTok account is connected (display name, avatar, open_id).
  • video.upload — to send finished clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
Clip Studio does not read your videos, followers, messages or analytics. Revoke access any time in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
