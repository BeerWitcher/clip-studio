Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

GTA Clip Studio (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a private, self-hosted tool operated by Yaroslav. It turns highlights of public gameplay streams into short vertical videos and uploads them to YouTube channels that the operator owns. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can revoke access.

Short version: the App only asks Google for permission to upload videos to your channel (youtube.upload). It does not read your emails, contacts, watch history, comments, subscribers or any other personal data, does not sell or share data, and shows no ads. You can revoke access at any time.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel you authorize. By connecting a YouTube channel to the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Google user data we access

DataWhyWhere it is kept
OAuth access and refresh tokens for the youtube.upload scope To upload videos to the authorized channel Only on the operator’s server, in a file readable by the operator alone
IDs of videos the App uploaded, their titles and upload time To avoid uploading the same clip twice and to respect daily limits A local upload log on the operator’s server

The App does not request, read or store: your name, email address, profile, contacts, channel analytics, watch history, comments, subscriptions, or any data of other YouTube users.

3. How we use the data

  • Solely to upload videos that the operator created with the App to the channel you authorized.
  • Not for advertising, profiling, analytics, selling, or training machine-learning models.
  • No human other than the operator has access to the tokens or the upload log.

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. Tokens are sent only to Google’s own servers when the App calls the YouTube API.

To edit clips, the App may send video content and transcripts of public gameplay streams (not Google user data) to processing services the operator chooses to enable:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to write video titles, descriptions and hashtags from a clip’s transcript;
  • ElevenLabs — to generate an optional voice-over from text the operator writes;
  • Twitch API — to find public streams and public viewer clips.

None of these services receive your Google account data or OAuth tokens.

5. Storage, security and retention

  • Tokens are stored on the operator’s server with file permissions restricted to the operator and transmitted only over HTTPS.
  • Tokens are kept until you revoke access or the operator disconnects the channel; they are then deleted.
  • The upload log keeps video IDs and titles for up to 12 months, then is deleted.
  • Downloaded stream footage is temporary working data and is deleted after the clips are rendered.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke the App’s access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings → Third-party access. After revocation the App can no longer upload anything.
  • To have the stored token and upload log deleted, email lytvynyaroslav9@gmail.com; we delete them within 7 days and confirm by email.
  • Videos already published stay on your channel until you remove them in YouTube Studio.

7. Creators whose streams are clipped

Clips are made from publicly available streams. Every clip credits the original creator on screen and in the description with a link to the source. If you are a creator and want your content removed or excluded from future clips, email lytvynyaroslav9@gmail.com — we remove it promptly and add your channel to an exclusion list.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
